Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Statufy ("we", "us") makes an iPhone app that turns two photos into a short "living statue" video. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Photos and face data

  • What we receive. The two photos you choose (for example, Mom and a child). You pick them one by one; the app never gets access to your whole photo library.
  • Before upload. Your iPhone checks that each photo has one face, using Apple’s on-device Vision framework. We re-encode the photo and remove location and other metadata.
  • What we do with them. We use the photos only to create the scene and video you asked for. Only after you tap “Allow and continue”, they are sent to fal.ai, a third-party AI service, and to the AI model providers it uses for this request (currently Google for the still image and safety check, and ByteDance for the video). They process the photos only to create your result and check it for safety.
  • No face recognition or profiles. We do not create face templates, biometric identifiers or profiles, we do not identify people, and we do not use your photos or videos to train AI models.
  • How long we keep them. Uploaded photos are deleted automatically within 24 hours. Generated previews and videos are deleted after 30 days. Our AI provider may keep request data for up to 30 days under its own policy.
  • Delete now. In the app, open Settings → “Delete my photos & videos”. Your photos, previews, videos and notification token are deleted immediately from our servers. We keep only purchase records (needed for your subscription) and an anonymous device counter used for preview limits. You can also delete a single video in My videos.
  • Consent. Upload only photos of people you have the right to use. For children, only your own child or with a parent’s permission. You can withdraw consent at any time: delete your photos and videos in Settings (this also resets the consent) or stop using the app.

What we collect

  • Photos and videos — described above. Used only to provide the app’s main feature.
  • An anonymous account ID — created automatically; we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Device identifiers — a one-way hash of the app’s vendor ID and an Apple DeviceCheck token, a hashed IP address. Used only to enforce free-preview limits and prevent abuse.
  • Purchase history — subscription and purchase status, processed by Apple and RevenueCat, used to unlock features and credits.
  • Product interaction — anonymous usage stats to improve the app (for example, “preview shown”, “video shared”) collected with TelemetryDeck, which does not use advertising IDs and does not identify you.
  • Push token — only if you tap “Notify me”, to tell you when a video is ready.

Service providers

  • fal.ai — AI image and video generation, content safety checks.
  • Supabase — database, file storage and server functions.
  • Fly.io — video finishing (adding the ending and watermark).
  • RevenueCat and Apple — purchases and subscriptions.
  • TelemetryDeck — privacy-friendly analytics.
  • Apple Push Notification service — notifications.

They process data only on our behalf and only to provide these services.

Children

Statufy is made for parents. It is not directed to children and children should not use it on their own. Photos of children are uploaded by their parents and are handled as described in “Photos and face data”.

Reports and safety

You can report any result in the app. Reports and repeated attempts to upload unsafe content are reviewed and can lead to blocking a device.

Your rights

You can delete your photos and videos at any time in the app. For any other request (access, correction, deletion), write to aavarija@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
