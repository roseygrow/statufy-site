Frequently asked

How do credits work?

1 credit = 5 seconds of video. A 10, 15 or 20-second video uses 2, 3 or 4 credits. Up to 3 watermarked previews a day are included; more previews use 1 credit each. Subscription credits refresh every week or month; purchased credit packs never expire.

My video failed. Did I lose credits?

No. If a preview or video fails on our side, its credits are returned automatically.

How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Your subscription and purchased credits move to the new device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Statufy, or tap “Manage subscription” in the app’s Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my photos and videos?

In the app open Settings → “Delete my photos & videos”. Everything is removed from our servers immediately. Uploaded photos are deleted automatically within 24 hours anyway, and videos after 30 days.

Something looks wrong or inappropriate

Tap “Report” under the video, or write to us. We review every report.
